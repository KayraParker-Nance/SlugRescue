# SlugRescue
A game where you collect sea slugs by rescuing them from habitats in danger, which can later be revitalised in order to release the slugs and restore the wild population. Sea slugs are kept in your sanctuary, where they are cared for and fed.

The main gameplay will occur in the sanctuary where players can care for and breed their collected slugs. There will be the option to send expeditions to habitats in danger to rescue and discover new slug species. Once enough species from a specific habitat have been discovered, the player will have the option to restore the habitat and return the captive-bred slugs to their natural habitat.

As the player progresses, more rare slugs and habitats will become available, which will require more care and resources.

The art style will be decided on later; mobile is the initial target platform
